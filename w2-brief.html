Two kinds of blank — never merged
null
Cannot apply.
Pants have no neckline.
"unknown"

Collapsing these lets a model earn credit for calling a missing fact inapplicable. Abstention has to cost something.
What these numbers are not
78 of 4,500 evaluation cells were human-reviewed. The rest still equal the frontier model's own answer. So absolute scores are partly tautological — but deltas under the same frozen evaluator stay honest. That's the only claim this project makes.

Twelve of 15 fields improved. Three didn't — which is exactly why per-field results get inspected instead of hiding behind one average.
What epoch 2 did not fix
The model has the JSON shell. It hasn't fully learned that every value comes from a closed list.
"woven""patchwork" "snap""asymmetric" "scarf""popovers" ["gather"]
Perfectly reasonable English. Not in the contract. That gap — semantically plausible vs. contract-valid — is the whole reason this task makes a good RL testbed.
Invalid-value fields across the 36 non-compliant records. Plus 15 rule violations, led by solid_is_not_multicolour (5) and auto applies-to (6).
